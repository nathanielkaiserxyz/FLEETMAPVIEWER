FLEETMAP VIEWER

Upload a CSV with 6 columns:

MODEL | SERIAL | ID | CUSTOMER | BUILDING | CUSTOMER NAME

The first four columns are primarily for internal use, and the last two are for customer understanding.

Explanation of the last three columns:

  • Customer: The specific name, e.g., customer serial. This is not used in the output and is for diagnostics only. Example: forklift: 30778Z
  • Building: The high-level grouping that the customer will recognize, e.g., SM BUILDING, SL. If there is one building you can use: ADMIN, JANITOR
  • Customer Name: The specific entity inside the grouping, e.g., Security Basestation, Security Handheld, Coordinators Office.
