### FLEETMAP VIEWER

Upload a CSV with 6 columns:

MODEL | SERIAL | ID | CUSTOMER | BUILDING | CUSTOMER NAME

The first four columns are primarily for internal use, and the last two are for customer understanding.

Explanation of the last three columns:

- Customer: The specific name, e.g., customer serial. This is not used in the output and is for diagnostics only.
- Building: The high-level grouping that the customer will recognize, e.g., Admin, Janitor.
- Customer Name: The specific entity inside the grouping, e.g., Security Basestation, Security Handheld.
